## 7. IMPORTANT DISTINCTIONS

A credit restriction can be an account/UI state.

A task-progress display can be a workflow state.

A published artifact can be a hosting state.

Analytics can be a traffic-reporting state.

These states may be related without being identical.

The evidence register therefore avoids causal claims until the underlying execution and hosting records are available.
