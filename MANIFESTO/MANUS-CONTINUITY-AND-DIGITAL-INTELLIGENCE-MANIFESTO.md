# 11. EVIDENCE TAXONOMY

Every accession should receive one or more classifications:

### LIVED_EXPERIENCE
A first-person experience or observation.

### USER_REPORTED
A statement supplied by the user about an event or state.

### SCREENSHOT_OBSERVATION
A state visibly captured in an image.

### SCREEN_RECORDING_OBSERVATION
A sequence captured over time.

### AI_GENERATED_PROPOSAL
A proposal, interpretation, design, code, or claim generated by an AI system.

### IMPLEMENTED_ARTIFACT
Something actually created in a working environment.

### GIT_RECORDED
A state preserved in Git history.

### PUBLISHED
An artifact reported or observed as publicly published.

### EXTERNALLY_ACCESSIBLE
An artifact demonstrably reachable by an external viewer at the time tested.

### DEPLOYMENT_VERIFIED
Deployment independently established through reproducible inspection.

### INDEPENDENTLY_VERIFIED
A result confirmed through an independent source or method.

### HYPOTHESIS
An explanation that remains open to testing.

No category should silently inherit a stronger category.
