## Why this crosswalk matters

These are not interchangeable artifacts. One is a completed repository monument; one is a user-supplied catalog entry linked to a Gemini share; one is a technical blueprint whose operational claims need external evidence; and two are titles whose source files have not yet been recovered.

The Observatory will preserve those distinctions while connecting the records. No source title will be upgraded to a completed artifact, and no blueprint field such as `deployment_status: LIVE` will be treated as independent proof of operation.
